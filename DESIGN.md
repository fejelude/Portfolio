# Itsmefeje design reference

## Overview

The homepage pairs Fejelude’s introduction with one of his own game captures. A visitor can meet Sofra, open the gallery, or read about the work behind both. Use captions to identify the game or project. Keep Fejelude’s first-person voice and the details specific to his work.

The gallery reads like a collection of game memories. The learning section reads like notes, and the roadmap stays in sequence. Sofra’s rose palette comes from her artwork. The birthday, album, and compendium use their own pixel and scrapbook language.

## Colors

Slate surfaces give the game captures room for their original color. Amber marks portfolio actions and keyboard focus. Sofra uses rose for the same roles. Keep secondary labels readable on both surfaces and give service and form states text labels.

## Typography

Barlow Condensed in bold carries names and display headings. Public Sans handles prose, navigation, and controls. Both are self-hosted Latin WOFF2 files with local Open Font License notices. Body copy starts at 16px and stays within 66 characters per line where practical. Small print is reserved for metadata. Use tabular numerals for counts and times.

## Layout

The public pages share a 78rem reading width and fluid side margins. The homepage puts the name and capture side by side at wide sizes and stacks them in that order below 44rem. Gallery titles and captions stay visible through every breakpoint. The admin table has a labeled, independently scrollable region on small screens.

Use section backgrounds to separate major changes in subject. Use rows for comparisons or tasks, numbered lists for actual sequences, and outlined surfaces for things a visitor can open or operate.

## Elevation & Depth

Use a surface change or a rule to identify a boundary. The gallery viewer dims the page behind it while it holds focus. Gameplay artwork and the birthday illustrations provide their own depth.

## Shapes

Public frames and primary controls use a 6px radius. Sofra’s settings surfaces use 10px. The gift pages keep the stepped edges on their pixel controls.

## Components

- The amber or rose button leads to the main action. Underlined text links handle secondary navigation. Public controls have a visible focus outline and a 44px minimum touch area.
- The compact menu uses a disclosure button and restores focus when closed with Escape. Its links stay available when scripts are disabled.
- The gallery uses native links to full-size images and a native dialog when scripts are available. The viewer supports arrows, touch swipe, Escape, and visible buttons. It announces the active image and restores focus to the opener.
- Sofra’s public portrait and dashboard icon animations start on request. The dashboard uses static posters at rest. The homepage surprise retains its audio timing and reduced-motion sequence.
- Admin sign-in accepts typing, paste, and password-manager entry. The optional numeric keypad updates the same input. Filter and search controls have visible labels.
