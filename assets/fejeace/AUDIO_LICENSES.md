# FejeAce audio notices

The FejeAce sound effects are selected from the following Kenney asset packs and converted from OGG to MP3 for broad browser support:

- [Casino Audio 1.1](https://kenney.nl/assets/casino-audio)
- [Interface Sounds 1.0](https://kenney.nl/assets/interface-sounds)
- [Impact Sounds 1.0](https://kenney.nl/assets/impact-sounds)
- [Music Jingles 1.0](https://kenney.nl/assets/music-jingles)

Created and distributed by [Kenney](https://kenney.nl). All four packs are released under [Creative Commons Zero (CC0 1.0)](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). Attribution is not required, but is included here with appreciation.

No audio from Super Ace or another proprietary slot game is included.
