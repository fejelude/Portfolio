Roblox Creations

My Roblox Creations.

These are the games I've made so far. A lot of my time, effort and heart went into both of them, and I wanted to share a little of the story behind each one.

01 / My first ever game

Dream Garage Tycoon

Roblox · Car collecting & tycoon

This was the first game I've ever made. I spent so much time trying to make it look good, making sure everything worked, and putting as much effort as I could into it. I even remember telling my parents that maybe one day, if people loved my games, I could help give us a more comfortable life.

But when the game launched, it didn't get nearly as many players as I had hoped. I cried while making this game, and it was hard to accept that even after giving something my best, things might not go the way I imagined.

Even so, I'm still proud of what I made. I guess this game was my stepping stone to creating more games, and it'll always mean something to me. :)

Play on Roblox ↗

02 / My second game

Build to Impress

Roblox · Voxel building

This is the second game I've made! It's inspired by Minecraft, but on Roblox. You get your own plot where you can build whatever you want out of blocks, let other players visit, and show them what you're capable of creating.

Something I'm really proud of is that I modeled all the assets myself, including the blocks, furniture and the other things you can build with. I wanted to make something I'd genuinely enjoy playing myself, and hopefully something other people would enjoy too.

Making this game after my first one reminded me that I still love creating, even when things don't always work out.

Play on Roblox ↗
