# Visual fidelity overhaul
High is the initial preset; existing explicit watch choices remain respected. All six worlds share a local preference, while the watch and ten-car collection have independent selectors. Keep mobile opt-in loading, visibility suspension and bounded render targets. The mechanical changes are an illustrative kinematic model, not a manufacturing CAD model or a certified escapement simulation. A nominal 250° balance amplitude, five beats per second and a one-minute cage preserve the original tooth ratios; the ribbon is a visual approximation, not a spring stress calculation. The astronomical displays remain artistic gear-driven representations, not an ephemeris. The car meshes are original stylized concepts, not manufacturer-accurate CAD.

Research: [FHH tourbillon mechanism](https://www.hautehorlogerie.org/en/watches-and-culture/watchmaking-knowledge/encyclopedia/tourbillon-complications), [IWC fixed fourth wheel and carriage](https://www.iwc.com/ww-en/journal/the-flying-tourbillon-where-time-flies), [amplitude reference](https://horologyatlas.com/academy/amplitude-and-what-changes-it), [watch finishing glossary](https://www.thewatchpages.com/watch-finishings-and-decorative-techniques). Polished shoulders, annular jewels, roller table and perlage-inspired plate work are visual interpretations.
