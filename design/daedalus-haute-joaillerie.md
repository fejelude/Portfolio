# Daedalus — Haute Joaillerie / Celestial Atelier

## Design
A rose-gold coronet of 112 round brilliants, with gold collets and four bead prongs per stone; a stationary channel of 48 graduated blue-to-aqua step-cut sapphires; eight stellar diamonds; 12 symmetric dial accents. The original 16-fold moon cut has nine latitude rings and planar end tables (284 triangles). The existing Natural Earth geography is retained with darker enamel and a warmer gilt finish. Long satellite arms gain lancet-shaped openwork, retaining the previous outer profile and endpoints. The caseback and dial use filtered engine-turning, the plate a filtered aventurine approximation, and circular brushing contrasts with polished edges. Two narrow studio reflection strips supplement existing lighting. No third-party imagery or branding, camera changes, or new user-facing controls.

## Mechanical invariants
See [transmission](daedalus-timekeeping-transmission.md) and [movement](daedalus-astral-grande-complication.md) for tooth counts, pitch radii, centre distances, gear indexing, frames, shaft locations and bevel geometry. The original movement and timekeeping modules are unchanged. Six beats/s advance a 15-tooth wheel half a pitch per beat. Fixed 96:8 interface gives the 60-second cage; reversing 16:80 and 16:64 stages give 300-second yoke and 1200-second carrier. Earth and moon retain their opposite signed 60-second spins and crossed axes. Timing train 24→20→24 then 38→18→40; fixed-sun counterrotation 24→24→24. Relative feed is 19/20 minus −1/20 = 1 cage revolution per minute. Equal 20:20 bevel reverses it; seconds-to-minutes 1/60, minutes-to-hours 1/12. All shaft locations, pitch geometry, hand parents and animation updates remain unchanged.

The stationary bezel gallery lies outside the sapphire wall, chapter and stellar settings are fixed to the plate, and the symmetric dial accents fit inside its existing perimeter. Moon radius does not exceed the old .405-unit envelope. Bridge apertures remove material without widening the outer shape; not a stress-certified redesign.

## Optical model
Hero moon uses physical transmission, IOR 2.41 and controlled roughness; small dispersion only if supported by the vendored engine. Smaller stones use opaque faceted high-index environment approximations (sapphire IOR 1.76). Not multi-bounce ray tracing or optical grading. Gem/setting/prong families are instanced, surface finishes derivative-filtered, and existing adaptive pacing and disposal retained.

## Verification and limitations
Existing tests are retained, new tests cover facet winding, finite normals, moon radius and gemstone spacing. The existing CI comparison now runs for this branch, capturing matched baseline/feature desktop/mobile overview, top, rear, side, dial, gears, orbit, tourbillon, escapement and case/lug views, including draw calls, triangles and synchronous GPU readback measurements. The agent environment could not clone GitHub or access the deployed site in a browser, so a pre-edit screenshot was not captured and the baseline is taken from the immutable base checkout by CI. Browser images, frame pacing and CI results require review before merging. Exact contact stresses, full collision avoidance, dynamic balance, manufacturing tolerances and actual gem optics are not validated.
