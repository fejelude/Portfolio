Rules & Paytable

How wins work

Matching symbols must begin on Reel 1 and continue through at least three consecutive reels. Every matching position multiplies the number of winning ways. Jokers substitute for normal symbols, never Scatters.

Bet × symbol payout × winning ways × cascade multiplier

Cascades and Golden symbols

Winning symbols disappear and only empty positions refill. A winning Golden symbol becomes a sticky Joker for the next evaluation instead of disappearing.

Free Spins

Three or more Scatters award 10 Free Spins. Three or more during the feature award +5, with no retrigger limit. Free Spin cascade multipliers are ×2, ×4, ×6, then ×10.

Symbol payouts

No real-money gambling: all peso amounts are fictional, session-only display values with no cash value.
