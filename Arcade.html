Rules & Paytable

🎴 Getting a win

Basically, wins start from the first reel. Get the same symbol across 3 or more reels and you win. The more copies you land on each reel, the more ways that win counts — all the way up to 1,024 ways. Jokers can stand in for normal symbols, but not Scatters.

Bet × symbol payout × winning ways × cascade multiplier

That is the exact math used for every win. One spin can pay up to 10,000× your bet, and that is the hard limit.

💥 Wins keep cascading

When you win, those symbols pop away and new ones drop into the empty spots. If the new cards make another win, the multiplier climbs. Base-game cascades go ×1 → ×2 → ×3 → ×5 (and stay at ×5 after that).

✨ Golden cards & Jokers

Golden Ace, King, Queen, and Jack cards mainly show up on reels 2–4. If a Golden card is part of a win, it does not disappear — it turns into a sticky Joker for the next cascade instead.

🎁 Free Spins

Land 3 or more Scatters and you get 10 Free Spins. Land 3+ again while they are running and another 5 spins are added. You can keep retriggering them. Free Spin cascades are juicier too: ×2 → ×4 → ×6 → ×10.

You can also use Buy Bonus. One bonus costs 40.5× your current bet and gives 10 Free Spins. Buy more than one if you want; your chosen bet stays locked until those spins finish.
