A little note about visitors.

I use basic visitor analytics to see which public pages people visit and what kind of device and browser they're using. The new visitor records include a masked IP network prefix, not your full IP, a random browser identifier, and the visit time. No clicks, search queries, exact locations, form contents or fingerprinting are collected by this visitor logger.

Matching visits are grouped within 30 minutes. The browser identifier is stored locally and rotates after 30 days. Clearing site storage resets it. Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control settings are respected. Private pages are not included. Visitors are estimated by browser, not identified as individual people.

The visitor history is access-restricted and stored within a configured record limit. Older records from the previous analytics system may still exist in storage. Separate security logging is retained for protecting the admin panel. Browser settings or network failures can prevent a visit from being recorded.
